Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

GTA6 Clip Bot is a private tool used only by its owner, Daniel Hatanian, to post popular, credited GTA 6 gameplay clips from Twitch streamers to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. Every post credits and links the original streamer.

Information we access

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): the owner's basic profile info (user.info.basic) and permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish) to the owner's own account.
  • YouTube (youtube.upload scope): permission to upload videos to the owner's own channel.
  • Instagram Graph API: permission to publish Reels to the owner's own account.

The tool does not access, collect or store data about any other users.

How information is used

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and used only to publish videos to the owner's own accounts. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data retention and deletion

No personal data from other users is retained. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Apps and services), at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and in Facebook/Instagram settings. Clips are removed on request from the original creator.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
