GTA6 Clip Bot

A private tool that posts popular, credited GTA 6 gameplay clips from Twitch streamers to my own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts. Every post credits and links the original streamer.

How it works

  1. Connect my TikTok account once with the button below (Login with TikTok).
  2. A scheduled job picks a top GTA 6 Twitch clip, reformats it to vertical and adds a credit to the streamer.
  3. The clip is posted to my TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API.
